Proprietary Evaluation Engine

Never Award Solely on Lowest Price.

In enterprise procurement, delivery reliability and supplier credibility matter as much as price. ProcureHub evaluates proposals against four key criteria:

40% Price Competitiveness: Strict Decimal comparison vs lowest bidder
25% Delivery Lead Time: Faster delivery schedules receive higher scores
20% Supplier Performance: Past delivery record and quality ratings
15% Payment Terms: Credit flexibility (Net 30/60 vs Advance)
